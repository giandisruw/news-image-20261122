## Stories used in the image

### 1. Drones set Russian ship Sparta II ablaze (LEAD)
- Sat 21 Nov morning (~08:40–08:50 local): two drones struck the
  Russian cargo ship Sparta II in international waters of the Gulf of
  Finland, west of Gogland island, about 3 nautical miles from Russia's
  maritime border. The ship caught fire, drifted into Finnish waters
  and anchored south of Loviisa; the blaze was still burning late on
  Saturday. All 16 crew evacuated to lifeboats, then to a nearby
  Russian ship, and were on their way back to Russia.
- The ship was bound from St Petersburg to the Russian exclave of
  Kaliningrad; it is US-sanctioned and owned by Oboronlogistika, a
  company that moves supplies for the Russian defence ministry, and
  may have been carrying military materiel (cargo unverified).
- Finnish armed forces spokesperson Aki-Mauri Huhtinen: "It is likely
  that Ukraine ... is behind (the attack)". Finland imposed temporary
  air and maritime restrictions in the eastern gulf; a Russian
  helicopter was reported ~4.1 km inside Finnish airspace near the
  ship. President Alexander Stubb called the events "serious".
- Sources: CNN (21 Nov), Reuters via WDSM710 (21 Nov), ISW Russian
  Offensive Campaign Assessment 21 Nov, NY Post (21 Nov), Helsinki
  Times (21 Nov).

### 2. Double-tap strike on Kryvyi Rih
- Friday night, reported Sat 21 Nov: a Russian attack on the centre of
  Kryvyi Rih, President Zelenskyy's hometown, killed two people — a
  7-year-old boy (died in intensive care of head injuries) and a
  20-year-old woman paramedic responding at the scene — and wounded 60,
  including six children. Two ballistic missiles struck first, then
  three more rockets hit doctors, rescuers and utility workers as the
  rescue operation began (a double-tap). About 25 apartment buildings,
  a hospital, schools, businesses and vehicles were damaged; four
  fires were started.
- Sources: AP via Netscape (21 Nov), NY Post (21 Nov), Al Jazeera via
  EuropeSays (21 Nov), Inshorts/Times Now (21 Nov).

### 3. Mexico captures "El Chapo Isidro"
- Friday night, announced Sat 21 Nov: Mexican special forces captured
  Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as "El Chapo Isidro", in Guasave,
  Sinaloa, using "stealth and surprise" and with US intelligence help.
  He is one of the FBI's 10 most wanted fugitives and leads the
  Los Mazatlecos cartel; the US had a $5 million reward for him.
- The operation set off a wave of violence: 11 people died — four
  Mexican soldiers and seven gunmen — with burning roadblocks, torched
  cars and helicopters firing over Guasave; fans fled a baseball game.
  He was flown to Mexico City on Saturday.
- Sources: Reuters (21 Nov), AP via WJSU/Reflector (21 Nov), CNN
  (21 Nov), Wall Street Journal (21 Nov).

### 4. Magnitude 6.9 earthquake in Peru
- Struck at 7:09 p.m. Lima time on Friday (00:09 UTC Saturday 21 Nov):
  a magnitude 6.9 earthquake (USGS; Peru's Geophysical Institute put
  it at 6.7–6.8, GFZ at 6.8) in the Andes, epicentre 34 km north of
  Junin at a depth of 108 km. It was felt in the capital, Lima, where
  residents fled into the streets. No damage or casualties were
  reported. The image uses the USGS figure of 6.9 and the UTC date.
- Sources: AP via Korea JoongAng Daily (21 Nov), Reuters via The
  Straits Times/redhot.sg (21 Nov), TBS News (21 Nov), VolcanoDiscovery
  (21 Nov).

### 5. COP31 runs past its deadline
- Sat 21 Nov, Antalya, Turkey: the UN climate summit went into overtime
  after its scheduled Friday close, with no final agreement. The latest
  draft (cut from 144 paragraphs to 80) contains no mention of coal,
  oil or gas, and dropped a proposed $120bn adaptation-finance target.
  EU Climate Commissioner Wopke Hoekstra: "It's either going to be a
  good deal or it's going to be no deal." Ethiopia (Addis Ababa) was
  announced as host of COP32 in 2027. The US did not take part directly.
- Sources: AP via LA Post/Netscape (21 Nov), Reuters (21 Nov),
  The Climate Watch (21 Nov), AsiaToday (21 Nov).

### 6. FBI crime database hacked
- First reported Fri 20 Nov by CNN, confirmed in a Fox News interview
  on Sat 21 Nov: hackers breached the FBI's National Crime Information
  Center (NCIC), the nationwide database police use for warrants and
  background checks. The FBI is investigating; what was taken is not
  yet clear. It is the second FBI breach in months, after the September
  ShinyHunters theft of employee data from an FBI jobs site. Trump:
  "I will say that it's not good. I don't like that."
- Sources: CNN (20–21 Nov), USA Today (21 Nov), Fox News (21 Nov),
  TechCrunch (21 Nov).

### 7. Asian markets plunge
- Fri 20 Nov: Japan's Nikkei 225 closed 5.06% lower (56,436.65),
  South Korea's Kospi fell 5.21% (4,869.96) and Taiwan's Taiex fell
  6.23% (36,033.29), as the AI sell-off spread from Wall Street
  (S&P 500 −3.7% on Friday, in correction). For the week: Taiex −17.5%
  (worst since 1990/1991), Nikkei −7.7%, MSCI Asia-Pacific ex-Japan
  −7% (worst since March 2020). SoftBank fell ~14.6%, Kioxia 11.3%.
- Sources: AsiaToday (21 Nov), One Turn daily round-up (21 Nov),
  FXStreet (20 Nov), TradeNewsBD/investingLive (21 Nov).

### 8. Basra power-station fire
- Sat 21 Nov ~06:00 local: a gas pipeline blast set the Hartha thermal
  power station north of Basra, Iraq, ablaze; the fire spread to
  nearby homes and vehicles. At least four people were killed and 35
  injured (early figures varied: 2 dead/28 injured, then 4/30–35);
  officials warned the toll could rise. The 800 MW station (running
  at ~400 MW) was shut down. Most casualties were residents suffering
  burns and smoke inhalation.
- Sources: Reuters via KRRO/WTBX (21 Nov), QNA via Fana News (21 Nov),
  QNC News (21 Nov), Inshorts (21 Nov).
